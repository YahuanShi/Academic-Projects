# Digital Image Processing

This repository contains four exercises completed for the **Digital Image Processing** course.
The course covers foundational and advanced techniques in digital image processing, including signal processing, image enhancement, restoration, feature extraction, and frequency-domain analysis.
---

## What this course covers

We learned things like:

- How images can be seen as signals
- How to clean up, fix, and enhance images
- How to detect edges, corners, shapes, and textures
- How to work in the frequency domain (yes, Fourier everywhere )
- How cameras form images and how to transform them
- Basics of feature extraction & segmentation

Basically: **why your phone camera is smart, and how to make computers see stuff in images.**

---

## Key Topics

- Fourier Transform & frequency domain
- Convolution & filtering (low-pass, high-pass, wavelets)
- Image enhancement & denoising
- Image restoration (inverse filter, Wiener filter, super-resolution)
- Edge detection & segmentation
- Mathematical morphology (skeletonization, medial axis, distance transform)
- Camera model & geometric transformations
- Feature extraction & texture analysis

---

## Exercises

Exercises focus on the *practical* side:

- trying variations and extensions
